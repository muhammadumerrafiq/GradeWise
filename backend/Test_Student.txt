My favorite memory was visiting the seaside cottage with my grandparents during the summer holidays. Every morning, the golden sunlight poured across the quiet beach while seagulls soared gracefully in the crisp breeze. We spent hours building intricate sandcastles and collecting colorful seashells along the shoreline. In the afternoons, my grandmother would bake warm cinnamon scones while grandfather taught me how to carve small wooden boats. Although the waves were sometimes too cold for swimming, we splashed happily near the edge until twilight painted the horizon purple. That magical summer filled my heart with warmth and joy.
