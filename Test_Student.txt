One of my favourite memories is the summer holiday when my grandfather taught me how to fish by the quiet lakeside. We woke up very early before sunrise when the misty fog was still hovering across the calm surface of the water. He handed me a polished wooden fishing rod that had belonged to his own father. Patiently, he showed me how to bait the small hook and cast the line into the deep water without creating any noisy splash. Although we only caught three small silver fish that entire morning, the peaceful silence and his encouraging words created an unforgettable bond that I will always cherish dearly.
